# ReplayLead Shipaton Strategy

Updated: 2026-09-01

## Decision

Build one excellent product story and pursue two evidence paths:

1. **Primary path:** regular Shipaton submission plus Influencer Career Coaching. Ship a polished public Play release, public source, a sub-two-minute video, and a thoughtful RevenueCat monetization flow.
2. **Grand Prize path:** pursue only if a live eligible store release can happen early enough to collect real RevenueCat revenue and defensible growth evidence.

Next Gen is conditional, not a default path: use it only if the entrant can document the required student status with an eligible academic email or equivalent proof.

## Why the playbook was narrowed

The original playbook spreads one month across Google Play, Galaxy Store, OneSignal, RevenueCat Ads, an AI backend, public growth, and several prize narratives. That increases integration and review risk without improving the three Career Coaching criteria.

The product now optimizes first for:

- realistic new-manager scenarios;
- active practice with credible pushback;
- useful, specific feedback;
- a visible confidence shift;
- the distinctive Rewind branching interaction;
- honest and testable RevenueCat monetization.

OneSignal, Galaxy-specific work, and RevenueCat Ads remain optional gates, not default scope.

## Non-negotiable evidence

- No invented users, revenue, conversion, testimonials, or deployed integrations.
- Every report must label whether it came from the cloud AI service or the on-device fallback.
- A store build must never contain a RevenueCat Test Store key.
- Grand Prize fields remain blank unless a live app and real RevenueCat revenue exist.
- Influencer materials must not use the influencer's name, likeness, voice, brand, or logo without written permission.

## Release gates

### Gate A - Submission materials ready

- Debug APK builds and runs.
- Rewind creates a real alternate branch.
- RevenueCat Test Store purchase and entitlement are demonstrated.
- Public repository includes source, setup instructions, and MIT license.
- Video and required icon/screenshot exist.

### Gate B - Live-store ready

- Play Console account status and production eligibility are verified from the actual account.
- Store listing, privacy disclosures, data safety, signing, and platform RevenueCat product are complete.
- Release build uses only the platform public SDK key.
- Purchase, restore, and entitlement behavior pass on the store test track.

### Gate C - Grand Prize credible

- The app is publicly downloadable in the United States.
- Real users and real RevenueCat revenue exist.
- Launch date, acquisition work, experiments, conversion, retention, and lessons are documented with evidence.
