New managers are expected to handle difficult feedback before they have ever practiced saying it. ReplayLead makes the real conversation your second attempt, not your first.

I start with a realistic workplace scenario and record how prepared I feel. The goal is explicit: name the impact, understand what happened, and agree on a concrete recovery plan.

This is active rehearsal. I have to write the words I would actually use, and the counterpart reacts to them. Advice becomes practice, not another article to read.

Here is the differentiator. Rewind returns to a specific moment and starts a true alternate branch. I can replace a vague response with clearer evidence, curiosity, support, and a concrete next step.

After the conversation, ReplayLead scores clarity, empathy, assertiveness, and actionability. It highlights one strength, one focused improvement, and stronger language I can carry into the real meeting. The confidence check makes the change visible.
