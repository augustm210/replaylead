# ReplayLead Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-31

ReplayLead helps people rehearse workplace conversations. This policy describes the intended production behavior of the Shipaton build. It must be reviewed against the deployed configuration before publication.

## Information processed

- Practice content: scenario choices and the conversation text a user enters.
- Coaching results: scores and suggestions generated from a practice session.
- Purchase information: RevenueCat processes app-user identifiers, product and entitlement status, and store transaction metadata. ReplayLead does not receive full payment-card details.
- Operational data: the cloud service may process IP addresses and basic request logs for security, rate limiting, and reliability.

## How information is used

Practice content is used only to generate the counterpart's response and coaching report. Purchase information is used to provide and restore paid features. Operational data is used to prevent abuse and diagnose service failures.

## Storage and retention

The current app keeps the active rehearsal in memory and does not upload practice history to a ReplayLead database. Conversation text is sent to the configured AI service when cloud coaching is enabled. Cloudflare and RevenueCat may retain service data according to their own terms and configured retention controls. This section must be updated if account-backed history is introduced.

## Sharing

Data is shared only with service providers needed to operate the app, currently RevenueCat for purchases and Cloudflare for the API and AI inference. ReplayLead does not sell personal information or use practice content for advertising.

## User choices

Users can use the on-device fallback when the cloud service is unavailable. Store purchase restoration is triggered only when the user selects **Restore purchases**. Users may stop a practice session at any time.

## Children

ReplayLead is designed for workplace use and is not directed to children under 13.
