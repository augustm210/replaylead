# ReplayLead Demo Script

Target length: 105–115 seconds. Record the real app and keep the final public video under two minutes.

## 0:00–0:12 — Problem

Voiceover: “New managers are expected to give difficult feedback before they have ever practiced saying it. ReplayLead makes the real conversation your second attempt, not your first.”

Show: home screen, then tap **Practice a conversation**.

## 0:12–0:28 — Set the scenario

Voiceover: “Choose a realistic moment and record how prepared you feel before starting.”

Show: **Give difficult feedback**, confidence 2, **Start rehearsal**.

## 0:28–0:52 — Active practice

Voiceover: “The counterpart reacts to what I actually say, so I have to practice the words—not just read advice.”

Type two concise manager responses. Pause long enough for the counterpart's replies to be legible.

## 0:52–1:10 — The differentiator

Voiceover: “If a response does not land, Rewind starts a real alternate branch from that moment.”

Tap the first **Rewind here**. Show **Branch 2**, then give a more curious and specific response.

## 1:10–1:29 — Coaching and confidence

Voiceover: “ReplayLead scores clarity, empathy, assertiveness, and actionability, then gives one strength, one focused improvement, and stronger language I can use. My confidence check makes the change visible.”

Show the full coaching report and confidence 2 → 4.

## 1:29–1:45 — RevenueCat

Voiceover: “RevenueCat powers ReplayLead Pro with a live localized Offering, entitlement verification, and user-triggered restore. The app never shows an invented price or ships a Test Store key in a release build.”

Show the configured paywall and a successful Test Store purchase/entitlement state.

## 1:45–1:55 — Close

Voiceover: “ReplayLead: practice the hard conversation, rewind the moment, and lead it better.”

Show: logo, tagline, public repository URL.

## Recording rules

- Replace every placeholder with a live, verified state before recording.
- Do not show keys, account IDs, email addresses, dashboards with private data, or fake metrics.
- Keep all taps deliberate; no dead time, notification banners, mouse cursor, or device frame.
- Use a public YouTube or Vimeo URL and verify it while signed out.
